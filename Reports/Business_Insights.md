# Key Business Insights

## Patient Demographics
- Gender distribution is balanced.
- Average patient age is approximately 52 years.

## Medical Conditions
- Six major medical conditions were identified.
- Patient counts are distributed fairly evenly.

## Billing
- Billing amounts vary significantly across patients.
- Some treatments have substantially higher average costs.

## Hospital Operations
- A small number of hospitals and doctors handle the highest patient volumes.

## Insurance
- Five insurance providers cover all patients.
- Provider distribution is relatively balanced.

## Admissions
- Admission trends can be monitored monthly to improve staffing and resource planning.
